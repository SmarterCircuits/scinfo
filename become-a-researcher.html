How it works

You're a tester, not a ghost writer.

Each project will be a little different, but the basic workflow goes like this...

Email me

If you're interested in becoming a boffin, email me and tell me about your skills and a little about you.


This all started because I was offered a product designed for a pet and I do not have any pets. The product was interesting and I realized that I could share a bit of the spoils of the channel and get a little help making content at the same time.-

Response

I will let you know if I'm not looking for testers or if for some reason it's not a good fit, that way you're not wondering.


If we move forward, I would need an address to send products to. These products would be coming from their manufacturer or their distrubtors directly. Some of these companies will not ship to P.O.Boxes, so that may limit some options, but I completely understand if you don't want to give out your address.

Match

If a product comes in that needs a specific type of equipment, environment or expertise that I do not have, I look for someone who is a good fit on my list of testers.


Priority will be closest fit and least worked with first to try to keep distribution even.

Planning

We will discuss what needs to be learned, what can realistically be tested, and how.


We'll go over any special privacy or logistics requirements as well as timelines, testing periods, and possible later follow-ups.

Testing and filming

When you receive the product, you'll need to film the opening of the product with or without yourself (in disguise, masked, or blocked). Then, proceed with product setup and testing, making notes and taking as much footage as is reasonable and according to the agreed on plan.

Report back

When the testing is done, even if there will be later follow ups, send the footage and notes to me via the agreed upon methods and then we'll have a follow up conversation where we can brainstorm the video approach.


Once we figure out a presentation, you'll need to then take any b-roll or pickup shots we discuss and send those to me. You don't need to do any more than just wave unless you want to. I need hero shots and demonstration, but those can be faceless if you prefer.

Posting and publishing

Once I'm done editing the video, I'll upload it somewhere for you to view it before it ever goes out. If there's a good reason to make any changes, we will. Then, the video may go to members only for a time before being ultimately made public.

Rewards

Unfortunately, I don't have a legal or logistical framework for sharing ad revenue or anything like that; However, as the channel grows, I plan to do that and possibly even more if it were to be successful enough. At the moment, the only reward is the product itself and a little fun.

Equipment: You will need a relatively decent camera. Most modern smart phones will do just fine. Even most old smart phones will do just fine. If you have more than one camera, that's even better. You don't need professoinal lights, but having bright light is critical to quality footage. I can offer some direction here where needed.
How you choose to be seen is up to you. If you'd like to where an elaborate costume, that's fine. If you'd like to only be a set of hands, that's okay too. I can even possibly help you set up a blocking shot like they do when they hide people's identities in documentaries, if you can scrounge up the appropriate types of lights and at leat one blocking flag--makeshift or otherwise.
